## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Search · August spam update is rolling out globally · Monitor**
Google began an all-language spam update on 18 August and says rollout may take a few days. Annotate organic reporting, isolate affected page groups and avoid broad corrective changes until the rollout settles.

**Evidence:** Confirmed on Google’s Search Status Dashboard; no impact pattern is established yet.  
[Direct source ↗](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/LEubPCm2octf2uMqCFKE)

**Retail media · ANA outlines a measurement-standardisation framework · Act**
ANA says inconsistent attribution windows leave marketers comparing methodology rather than performance. Record each network’s window, data source and incrementality method before comparing reported outcomes across retail media.

**Evidence:** Confirmed trade-body publication; the public summary supports the comparison problem, while the full interview requires login and was not independently tested here.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.ana.net/miccontent/show/id/pulse-2026-08-interview-jackson-bazley)

**Apple · German consent-prompt commitments become binding · Monitor**
Apple will make personalised-ad consent prompts for its own services and third-party apps more neutral, give publishers more scope to explain their model, and simplify combining platform and statutory consent requests. Map current German app flows and plan a controlled before-and-after test when the changes ship; do not assume a consent lift.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Germany’s competition regulator; implementation timing and performance effects remain unproven.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.bundeskartellamt.de/SharedDocs/Meldung/EN/Pressemitteilungen/2026/08_17_2026_Apple_ATTF.html)

Version 0.1.5 adds subfolder-scoped research and local-SEO MCP tools. Trial one public site in loopback Docker with a capped DataForSEO key; the useful core is open, but a 0.1.x release is not yet a replacement decision.
