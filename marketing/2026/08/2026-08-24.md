## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Ads · Attribution reports omit late offline conversions · Act**

Google says its attribution engine bypasses offline conversions uploaded more than seven days after the event even though standard reports still record them. Keep uploads below seven days and reconcile Campaigns columns with Model Comparison before judging attribution or bidding.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google Ads Help, but the page has no public revision date and alternates between conversion age and upload latency; treat seven days as the operational ceiling until Google clarifies the wording.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722023?hl=en)

**New Zealand · Government announces under-16 social-media bill · Monitor**

The Online Safety Bill is scheduled for introduction today, requiring high-risk platforms to age-check users and child-used services to assess and report risks, with penalties proposed up to 10% of global revenue. Map youth-audience and age-data dependencies now, but do not treat the proposal as an enacted targeting rule.

**Evidence:** Confirmed government announcement; parliamentary passage, final scope and commencement remain unresolved.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.beehive.govt.nz/release/government-moves-ban-u16s-social-media)

**AI-agent advertising · IAB measurement framework is due 12 November · Monitor**

IAB is drafting a shared approach to credit ads encountered by AI agents and to separate AI-created awareness or intent from AI-assisted decisions. Teams running early tests should preserve raw exposure evidence and avoid assigning conversion value until the framework and platform signals are public.

**Evidence:** Observed in Digiday reporting quoting IAB’s AI lead; the draft is not public, the working group is unnamed and key platform evidence does not yet exist.  
[Direct source ↗](https://digiday.com/media/the-iab-is-developing-a-framework-to-tackle-ai-advertising-measurement/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**twentyhq/twenty · Test carefully · AGPL-3.0 core; marked enterprise files separate · Mature, high-churn**

v2.34.0 fixes composite-only CRM updates that could persist without an internal event or timeline row on the production-default legacy ORM. In a disposable workspace, repeat empty-to-populated and populated-to-populated composite changes plus a scalar control before upgrading.

**Evidence:** Project release and merged tests; maintainers live-characterised the failure and added legacy/ORM-v2 integration coverage, but this run did not independently exercise the image.  
**Boundary:** Twenty Cloud places CRM data with the provider; self-hosting needs Docker, PostgreSQL, a durable encryption key, backups and HTTPS/private exposure. The database holds contact and deal data, while lost or exposed keys affect stored OAuth, application and TOTP secrets.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/releases/tag/twenty%2Fv2.34.0)
