## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Apple · Sign in with Apple relay addresses are changing domain · Act**

Later this year, new relay addresses will move from `privaterelay.appleid.com` to `private.icloud.com`; existing addresses will keep working. Lifecycle and commerce teams should update validation and allowlists, then test identity matching, CRM sync and email delivery before rollout.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Apple on 24 August; Apple separately says iCloud+ Hide My Email addresses will remain on `icloud.com`.  
[Direct source ↗](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=1ptvdtcm)

**Google Merchant Center · 500 × 500 product-image minimum starts 31 January 2027 · Act**

Commerce teams should inventory feed images below the new minimum, prioritise high-spend SKUs and retain 1500 × 1500 masters where possible. Google says smaller images will surface “Image too small” warnings.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google Merchant Center Help; 500 × 500 is the requirement, while 1500 × 1500 is a recommendation, and the help page carries no public revision date.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324350?hl=en)

**YouTube · Public view counts now start at the first frame · Act**

Public views now count when a video starts and can count replays, aligning longer videos with Shorts. Annotate 24 August as a measurement break, avoid pre/post comparisons of raw public views and use engaged views or watch time for performance decisions.

**Evidence:** Corroborated reporting cites YouTube’s update; public counts change, while Partner Program eligibility, earnings and engaged-view measures do not.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/youtube-changes-public-view-counts-1236679708/)

**twentyhq/twenty · Watch · AGPL-3.0 core; marked enterprise files separate · Mature, high-churn**

v2.34.2 made ORM v2 the only CRM workspace read/write path; v2.34.3 and v2.34.4 then fixed two cross-version upgrade failures. Existing self-hosters should clone a current backup into staging, run the full upgrade path and keep production pinned until records and workflows pass.
