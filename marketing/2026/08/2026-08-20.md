## 📡 Marketing Signals

**FTC · Draft policy targets undisclosed personalised pricing · Act**
The FTC opened comment on a draft statement saying retailers may mislead consumers when a price appears fixed but varies by individual, or personal data drives pricing without disclosure. Audit US price-personalisation inputs and require disclosure and legal review before rollout.

**Evidence:** Confirmed FTC proposal; it is not a final rule, and the 30-day comment window starts after Federal Register publication.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/08/ftc-seeks-comment-enforcement-policy-statement-regarding-personalized-pricing)

**UK pricing · CMA opens three drip-pricing investigations · Act**
The CMA is investigating Trainline, Virgin Atlantic and RED Driving School over whether mandatory fees appeared in the first price shown. Audit UK ads, landing pages and checkout feeds so unavoidable charges are upfront; preserve before-and-after evidence.

**Evidence:** Confirmed formal investigations under strengthened powers; no infringement finding has been made.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/trainline-virgin-atlantic-and-red-driving-school-investigated-for-drip-pricing)

**Microsoft Advertising · AI Max for Search begins global rollout · Test**
AI Max moves search expansion, generated text and final-URL expansion beyond pilot. Google Ads imports can carry settings across, while existing predictive matching and autogenerated text may appear enabled. Audit imports and run one experiment with brand, term and URL controls.

**Evidence:** Observed rollout and account-handling details reported by Search Engine Journal; Microsoft’s incremental value remains untested.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/microsoft-advertising-rolls-out-ai-max-globally/586459/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**growthbook/growthbook · Test carefully · MIT core; enterprise directories separate · Mature**
v5.0.1 fixes a project-rated critical authentication flaw affecting self-hosted non-SSO deployments with public `/auth/*` endpoints and adopts hardened images. Existing users should patch; new evaluators should trial a backed-up localhost deployment on one low-risk experiment.

**Evidence:** Project advisory, inspected licence and hardening docs; exploitability and patch behaviour were not independently tested.  
**Boundary:** Default Compose settings are for local development and expose app services; use private networking, unique JWT/encryption secrets and a least-privilege warehouse account before real data.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/growthbook/growthbook/releases/tag/v5.0.1)

**logly/mureo · Test carefully · Apache-2.0 · Experimental**
v0.10.48 records why a platform refresh failed and separates “not collected” from a real zero, reducing the risk that stale Meta or Google figures look current. Start with its synthetic demo or read-only import mode; do not infer that autonomous account changes are safe.

**Evidence:** Project release notes, repository documentation and inspected Apache-2.0 licence; no live-account or independent quality test was run.  
**Boundary:** Python 3.10+ and an agent host are required; live mode holds ad-platform credentials and can mutate accounts across a large tool surface, so keep the first trial read-only and require human approval plus rollback checks for every write.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/logly/mureo/releases/tag/v0.10.48)
