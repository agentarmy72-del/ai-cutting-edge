## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Local Services Ads · Missed and follow-up calls become billable on 1 October · Act**
Advertisers receiving Google’s notice face charges when a business-hours caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds without an answer, and for later valid calls after an unqualified first contact. Audit missed-call rates, staffing or IVR, dispute evidence and budget exposure now.

**Evidence:** Observed in a mandatory Google account notice reproduced by PPC Land and corroborated by Search Engine Land; Google has not published a public help-page update.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ppc.land/google-charges-local-services-advertisers-for-missed-calls-over-20-seconds/)

**TikTok Brazil · Regulator orders R$153.7m fine and ad suspension on the unregistered feed · Act**
ANPD found children’s data handled without a valid legal basis and requires deletion plus a compliance plan; the no-account experience must suspend ads and reduce data collection. Brazil teams should annotate expected reach changes and recheck age, consent and youth-data controls.

**Evidence:** Confirmed ANPD decision; ByteDance can appeal the fine, while the compliance plan is approved.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-multa-tiktok-em-r-153-7-milhoes-por-falhas-na-protecao-de-dados-de-criancas-e-adolescentes)

**X Ads · Official MCP gives agents 23 campaign tools, including 10 writes · Test**
Agents can read performance and create, edit or activate campaigns; new campaigns and line items start paused. Begin with `ads.read` in a separate app, log every change and keep activation and budget approval human before considering write access.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in X’s official documentation; paused creation limits immediate spend, but activation remains an exposed write tool.  
[Direct source ↗](https://docs.x.com/x-ads-api/mcp)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**vizuh/clicktrail-js · Test carefully · MIT · Experimental release candidate**
v0.1.0-rc.3 packages deterministic first- and last-touch capture, consent gates and Astro, Nuxt, n8n and Directus adapters. Trial a synthetic UTM/click-ID journey on one test site with grant, denial and withdrawal states; deterministic parsing is not proof of incrementality.

**Evidence:** Release and MIT licence inspected; the local build and all bundled workspace tests passed, but real-site accuracy was not benchmarked.  
**Boundary:** Node/TypeScript integration; first-party cookies still carry marketing identifiers and need lawful consent, retention and destination controls. Cross-domain continuity needs signing, and non-core entry points remain incubating.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/vizuh/clicktrail-js)

**opengrowhq/opengrow · Watch · AGPL-3.0 · Pre-alpha**
v0.2.0 adds an approval-gated content orchestrator, scheduled publishing, Stripe-linked attribution and 19 MCP tools. Trial only the five-service Lite stack with fixture content, a local model and publishing disabled; its closed-loop recommendations are project-claimed, not validated growth evidence.

**Evidence:** Project release, changelog and AGPL licence inspected; no independent runtime or model-quality test was completed.  
**Boundary:** Lite needs Docker and about 2 GB RAM, skips malware scanning and OpenFGA, and stores brand assets, tracking events, tokens and publishing credentials; production expands to 15 services and about 8 GB.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/opengrowhq/opengrow)
