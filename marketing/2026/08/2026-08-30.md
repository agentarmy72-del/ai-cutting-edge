## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Search · EEA site-reputation enforcement changes today · Act**
From 30 August, site-reputation manual actions still apply directly to affected sections outside the EEA but no longer affect EEA results; Google may instead separate sections so they rank without the host site’s reputation. Publisher and SEO teams should segment EEA/non-EEA visibility, review third-party content governance and use reconsideration or mediation where eligible.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google; the effect of independent section ranking is not yet measured.  
[Direct source ↗](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/08/update-site-reputation-policy)

**Meta teen accounts · judge-approved US controls constrain usage windows · Monitor**
In participating US jurisdictions, under-18s on Facebook and Instagram get a cumulative two-hour limit, a midnight-to-6am block, muted school-hour notifications and a non-algorithmic feed option; most terms last 10 years. Youth-facing teams should annotate the rollout and separate pre/post delivery rather than assume a reach effect.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Meta’s notice updated after judge approval; downstream reach and ad-delivery effects are unmeasured.  
[Direct source ↗](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/08/agreement-with-state-attorneys-general-supporting-teens/)

**YouTube Shopping · US creators can now tag Amazon products · Test**
Eligible US creators can tag Amazon products in Shorts, long-form videos and livestreams. Creator-commerce teams should pilot one creator and category, then reconcile YouTube clicks and sales with Amazon reporting before treating the integration as incremental performance.

**Evidence:** Confirmed availability; YouTube provides no performance benchmark.  
[Direct source ↗](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-shopping-amazon-creator-affiliates/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**formbricks/formbricks · Test carefully · AGPL-3.0 core; SDKs MIT and EE separate · Mature, active**
v5.4.1 fixes SSO recovery that left local authentication factors active, Microsoft multi-tenant SSO failures and adds callback-outcome logging. Trial synthetic respondents in staging and rehearse recovery and role access before upgrading.

**Evidence:** Project release and split licence inspected; no independent runtime test was run.  
**Boundary:** Hosted use places survey and contact data with the provider; self-hosting shifts app, database, authentication, TLS and backup duties to the operator. Keep live respondent data out of the trial.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks/releases/tag/5.4.1)

**every-app/open-seo · Test carefully · MIT · Early, active 0.1.x**
v0.1.6 combines keyword, rank, backlink and site-audit workflows with Google Analytics and MCP, but buys its core SEO data from DataForSEO rather than operating an independent index. Trial one public domain on loopback with a capped account and compare results with Search Console and manual checks.

**Evidence:** MIT licence and source inspected; all 1,113 bundled tests passed, but no live DataForSEO call or accuracy benchmark was run.  
**Boundary:** Docker mode disables app authentication, defaults to port 3001 and sends aggregate telemetry unless opted out. Keywords and domains go to DataForSEO; GA, Search Console, MCP and optional OpenRouter connections widen credential and data exposure.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/releases/tag/v0.1.6)
