## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Apple Maps · Ads are now delivering in the US and Canada · Test**

Local-search teams can buy Suggested Places and search-results inventory. Start with one locality and one defined outcome, keep calls, directions and site taps separate from store visits, and treat the launch credit as a trial subsidy rather than performance evidence.

**Evidence:** Confirmed on Apple Ads; reach and action-rate figures are platform-claimed, with no public CPC benchmark or independent performance data.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ads.apple.com/maps)

**TikTok · $400m COPPA settlement raises the youth-data bar · Act**

The US Justice Department settled litigation over children's privacy. Teams running youth-facing creative, audiences or creator activity should recheck age screening, consent, pixel and event data, and landing flows; the settlement itself is not a new targeting rule.

**Evidence:** Confirmed DOJ settlement; the resolved claims remain allegations, with no determination of liability, and DOJ says TikTok had already strengthened younger-user controls.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-secures-400m-settlement-tiktok-and-bytedance-resolve-childrens-privacy)

**Meta Ads · Ad-set placement and device exclusions are being removed · Monitor**

Ads Manager notices say advertisers will lose options to exclude individual placements, whole Meta apps, device classes and operating systems. Inventory-sensitive teams should document hard restrictions, review account-level controls and value rules, and recheck creative and brand-safety assumptions before the change lands.

**Evidence:** Observed in advertiser notices and specialist reporting; Meta has not published a public rollout date or full migration note.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/meta-remove-placement-controls-from-ad-sets-12401398)
