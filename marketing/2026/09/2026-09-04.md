## 📡 Marketing Signals

**YouTube branded content · automatic labels and market-level gates · Act**

YouTube now lets creators set country and minimum-age limits when declaring a brand deal, and may label undeclared deals detected by its systems. Put declaration, jurisdictional gating and evidence capture into every creator-upload checklist; detection does not transfer legal responsibility.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by YouTube; detection signals, false-positive rates and universal override access are not disclosed.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en)

**OpenAI Ads · small custom audiences can now suppress delivery · Test**

A beta-advertiser update reportedly removes the 25,000-match floor for exclusions—not inclusion—and expands list maintenance and identifier support. Trial one suppression use case, then verify match quality, consent and regional rules before using the wider identity surface.

**Evidence:** Observed in a beta update reported by PPC Land; OpenAI has not published matching public documentation, so scope and availability remain unconfirmed.  
[Accessible report ↗](https://ppc.land/openai-drops-25-000-user-floor-for-chatgpt-ad-exclusion-audiences/)

**Shopify POS · staff can see identified customers’ abandoned online carts · Act**

POS v11.14 lets permissioned staff view products from an eligible abandoned checkout once the customer and store location are identified; browsing history stays hidden. Audit role access, privacy wording and staff scripts before enabling it, then test a web-to-store handoff with a synthetic account.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Shopify; the feature is permission-gated and Shopify supplies no conversion evidence.  
[Direct source ↗](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/permitted-store-staff-can-now-see-an-identified-customer-s-online-cart-in-pos)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**vendurehq/vendure · Test carefully · GPL-3.0-or-later or commercial · Stable 3.7.3**

The patch fixes eleven disclosed flaws—four critical—including external/SSO customer takeover and cross-channel refund control. Upgrade all Vendure packages together in staging; test external registration and channel-scoped admin, payment and refund paths.

**Evidence:** Tagged release, public advisories and actual GPL licence with plugin exception inspected; no exploit or runtime test was run here.  
**Boundary:** No database migration is required, but old job records may retain session tokens: purge settled jobs and consider invalidating administrator sessions. Set an explicit CORS allowlist and regression-test tightened permissions.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/vendurehq/vendure/releases/tag/v3.7.3)

**activepieces/activepieces · Test carefully · MIT core; enterprise code separate · Maturing 0.90.1**

The release adds approval-email attachments, Clay and Form.io connectors, MCP grant controls, and correct HTTP 500 responses for failed synchronous webhooks. Trial a synthetic approval and deliberately failed webhook before moving an existing automation.

**Evidence:** Tagged 0.90.0/0.90.1 releases, source and split licence inspected; behavior was not independently exercised.  
**Boundary:** Self-hosting needs Docker, PostgreSQL and Redis and centralizes workflow payloads and service credentials. Review worker sandbox and network settings, community connectors and every webhook before exposure.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/activepieces/activepieces/releases/tag/0.90.1)
