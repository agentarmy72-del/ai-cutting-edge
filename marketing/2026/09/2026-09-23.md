**Shopify Analytics · session metrics start a new baseline · Act**

Annotate 21 September in dashboards; do not attribute shifts in conversion, bounce or add-to-cart rates to campaign performance alone.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Shopify on 21 September; orders, sales and customer counts are unchanged.  
[Direct source ↗](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/shopify-analytics-session-measurement-improvements)

**IAB Tech Lab · AAMP 3.0 drafts agent-readable media proposals · Monitor**
OpenProposal defines packages and RFP responses that buying agents can compare against a brief and hand into existing execution standards. Map which brief, suitability, price and approval decisions must remain human-controlled; do not build production workflows yet.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in the official draft repository; it says the standard is unratified, its schema is unpublished and its licence remains unresolved.  
[Direct source ↗](https://github.com/IABTechLab/OpenProposal)

**IAB · common legal terms proposed for measurement services · Monitor**
The Measurement Services Addendum offers one modular contract for measurement, verification, attribution and analytics across advertisers, agencies, publishers, platforms and providers. Compare current scopes, data duties and dispute terms before the 22 October comment deadline.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by IAB on 22 September; v1.0 is a 30-day public-comment draft, not final terms.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.iab.com/guidelines/iab-releases-measurement-services-addendum-for-public-comment/)
