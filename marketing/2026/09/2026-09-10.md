## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Search · EEA commercial results split aggregators from suppliers · Act**
Google now shows an aggregator unit for EEA hotel, flight, long-distance transport and product queries, with the top-ranked service expanded by default, plus a supplier unit for direct providers. Aggregators need approval and feed/API readiness; suppliers need crawlable inventory and segmented EEA reporting.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google Search Central, updated 8 September; no independent traffic-impact data.  
[Direct source ↗](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/aggregator-unit)

**UK digital ads · Clearcast moves compliance review upstream · Test**
Clearcast launched iClear to pre-screen scripts, images and video against the CAP Code, plus allClear for human review across digital channels. Use AI only as early triage while assets can change; reserve expert review for high-risk or final calls.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Clearcast; iClear accuracy is unvalidated, and its output is not an ASA ruling or legal guarantee.  
[Direct source ↗](https://clearcast.co.uk/news-and-blog/clearcast-expertise-for-digital-ads-the-launch-of-iclear-and-allclear/)

**IPA TouchPoints · younger commercial-media time is increasingly solo and mobile · Test**
IPA reports that 16–34s spend 46% of commercial-media time alone; smartphones and social plus online video each account for 49%, while TV sets fell from 32% in 2015 to 21%. Refresh planning assumptions, but test outcomes rather than treating time share as a budget instruction.

**Evidence:** Observed in the 2026 GB TouchPoints analysis published 9 September; it measures media behaviour, not campaign effectiveness.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ipa.co.uk/news/making-sense-2026/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**logly/mureo · Test carefully · Apache-2.0 · Beta, high-churn 0.17.4**
v0.17.4 stops uncertain or wrong-grain findings becoming asserted top risks and makes partial Google or Meta account lists explicit. Re-run its synthetic demo and compare each flag with its underlying row before considering read-only BYOD.

**Evidence:** Release, changed files and licence inspected; version/help and demo initialization ran here, and 288 focused tests passed. No live account was tested.  
**Boundary:** Python 3.10+ and an agent host are required. BYOD prevents account writes but stores exports locally; live mode holds credentials and exposes mutating ad tools. The security policy still names 0.10.x as supported.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/logly/mureo/releases/tag/v0.17.4)

**Boundary:** Self-hosting needs PostgreSQL, Redis, secrets and local or S3 storage; real sends and events cross SES or Resend. Contact data is sensitive, while Enterprise-marked files and MIT packages sit outside core AGPL.
