## 📡 Marketing Signals

**EU KIDS Act · youth access and profiling restrictions enter legislative process · Monitor**
The Commission proposes no social access below 13, parent-controlled accounts at 13–14, autonomous accounts from 15, age assurance and no profiling-based recommender feeds for minors. Map youth-audience campaigns and contextual alternatives now, while plans and platform products can still be redesigned.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by the European Commission on 17 September; this seven-day regulatory exception is actionable now, but Parliament and Council may change the proposal and no duties are yet in force.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1890)

**Apple ATT · alternative EU consent prompt reaches iOS 27.2 · Act**
Apple says developers may use an alternative ATT prompt across the EU; Germany, France, Italy, Poland and Romania get only that version. With 27.2 in beta, app-growth teams should update market-specific QA and consent records without assuming the new wording will lift opt-ins.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Apple on 16 September; this is a material implementation of its earlier competition commitments, while the underlying permission trigger is unchanged and outcomes are untested.  
[Direct source ↗](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=idsft9ai)

**Google Ads · AI video dubbing appears in Asset Studio · Test**
Select advertisers reportedly can translate and dub existing video into 33 languages and locales. Test one non-sensitive asset, require native-speaker review of claims, voice and timing, and keep localised approval separate from media buying.

**Evidence:** Observed in current trade reporting on 19 September; Google’s public Asset Studio page does not yet document dubbing, eligibility or quality controls.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.contentgrip.com/google-ads-ai-dubbing/)
