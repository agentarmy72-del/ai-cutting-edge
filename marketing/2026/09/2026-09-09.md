## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Ads · local-intent Performance Max is offline-goal only · Test**
Google added Local customer optimization to store-goal Performance Max, prioritising near-location intent across Maps, Waze and local Search. It cannot coexist with online goals or Merchant Center products; isolate one store-only pilot and judge incremental visits or sales.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in current Google documentation; benefits are platform-claimed, with no performance evidence.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17198659?hl=en)

**Shopify and Meta · products enter the AI channel by default · Act**
Meta now appears in Shopify’s Agentic Storefronts, with products shared through Shopify Catalog by default and separate controls for catalogue access and direct checkout. Review permissions and product eligibility; record a baseline before treating this channel as incremental.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Shopify’s 8 September changelog; no reach or conversion evidence.  
[Direct source ↗](https://changelog.shopify.com/posts/meta-is-now-an-ai-channel-in-your-admin)

**UK retail media · attributed sales still are not comparable across networks · Act**
ISBA and MediaSense checked 200+ capability points across five large networks and found different sale definitions, attribution methods, reporting and service tiers. Require each network’s definitions, windows, buying tier and exportable report before comparing ROAS.

**Evidence:** Observed through advertiser-funded platform demonstrations, document review and structured validation; the study sets a baseline, not a performance league table.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.isba.org.uk/article/isba-and-mediasense-report-establishes-first-capability-baseline-uk-digital-retail-media)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**squirrelscan/squirrelscan · Test carefully · MIT · Maturing, high-churn 0.0.93**
v0.0.93 reuses rule results for unchanged pages, cuts project-measured light startup memory from ~140 MB to ~43 MB and extends slow-origin fallback. Pin it, run a one-page offline audit, then compare one warm re-audit before delivery use.

**Evidence:** Tagged release, licence and checksum installer inspected; two one-page offline audits passed here, with the second replaying the unchanged page. Large-site figures remain project-claimed.  
**Boundary:** Node 18+ installation downloads a native binary and stores crawl history locally; telemetry and update checks default on outside offline mode. Cloud rendering or publishing sends page or report data to the service.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/squirrelscan/squirrelscan/releases/tag/v0.0.93)

**miralemalic/bouncesitrep · Test carefully · MIT · Experimental, untagged v2.0.0**
This one-file Python tool turns campaign bounce text into data, reputation, authentication, content, recipient and transient queues. Trial it on a synthetic or redacted export, then manually check every non-high-confidence decision before generating suppressions.

**Evidence:** Source and licence inspected; 101 bundled tests and a synthetic five-row CSV trial passed here. Its 104,690-message corpus and 99.5% coverage remain project-claimed because the corpus is not public.  
**Boundary:** It makes no network calls, but exports can contain addresses and sending-infrastructure clues; keep them out of model prompts. Its English, US cold-email corpus requires separate validation for transactional or non-US mail.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/miralemalic/bouncesitrep)
