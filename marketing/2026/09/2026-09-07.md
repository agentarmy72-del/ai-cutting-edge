## 📡 Marketing Signals

**YouTube sponsorships · co-viewed TV metric is private and modelled · Act**
YouTube now estimates total people watching a TV playback, but the number is private to creator analytics, takes up to 48 hours to settle and does not affect public views or payouts. For sponsorships, require an export ending two days ago and separate co-viewed exposure from engaged views.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by YouTube’s Creator Insider; model inputs are broad, while error bands and advertiser-side verification are undisclosed.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8DgguXIU4L8)

**Google Video campaigns · three-tier lookalikes extend to brand buys · Test**
Google says Video brand campaigns can use first-party seeds above 100 matched people and choose 2.5%, 5% or 10% reach tiers; segments refresh every one to two days. Pilot one high-intent seed, create it early and judge incremental conversion quality—not reach alone.

**Evidence:** Confirmed current Google documentation; seven-day rollout exception because the control is live for campaign setup now. Google provides no dated changelog or performance evidence.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17219920?hl=en)

**TikTok Shop US · packaging rules ban review-bait and promotional inserts · Act**
A new US policy lists ten prohibited packing practices, including unsolicited products, review incentives and unauthorised promotions, plus category-specific duties. Remove inserts from fulfilment specs and audit 3PL evidence before the next dispatch.

**Evidence:** Observed in an accessible report of TikTok’s 3 September policy; seven-day rollout exception because it changes current fulfilment. One bulky-item threshold note remains unresolved.  
[Accessible report ↗](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shop-bans-review-bait-inserts-in-10-packing-rules/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**squirrelscan/squirrelscan · Test carefully · MIT · Maturing 0.0.90**
v0.0.90 makes site audits back off on rate limits, marks lost coverage partial instead of broken, keeps body timeouts armed and adds compression and secret-leak checks. Run a one-page no-telemetry audit on a test site, then compare findings manually before using it for delivery QA.

**Evidence:** Tagged release and licence inspected; a local no-account v0.0.90 trial completed one page and wrote JSON, but finding accuracy was not benchmarked.  
**Boundary:** The local CLI crawls the target over the network; telemetry is on by default but can be disabled. Hosted MCP, cloud audits and report publishing extend the data and authentication boundary.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/squirrelscan/squirrelscan/releases/tag/v0.0.90)

**Evidence:** Tagged release and actual mixed licence inspected; no runtime send was performed.  
**Boundary:** Self-hosting uses Docker, PostgreSQL and Redis and centralises contacts, email/calendar data and provider credentials; optional S3, Gmail, Microsoft and SMTP extend the trust boundary.
