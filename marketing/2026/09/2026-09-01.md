**Amazon Ads · regulators challenge undisclosed auction surcharges · Act**
The FTC and 22 states allege Amazon advertised generalized second-price auctions while adding a hidden “soft reserve price”; its complaint says Sponsored Products advertisers paid their own winning bid about 80% of the time in 2024. Export bid, CPC and search-term history, flag models that assume GSP pricing, and await Amazon’s response before changing live strategy.

**Evidence:** Confirmed filing; the conduct and “tens of billions” impact are allegations, not findings, and no remedy or platform change exists yet.

**Evidence:** Confirmed rollout; the reported acquisition and ROAS gains are platform/customer case-study claims, not independent benchmarks.  
[Direct source ↗](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/blog/unified-attribution-globally-available)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**dgerridge/email-qa-framework · Try · MIT · Early 0.1 reference implementation**
This new local CLI checks campaign JSON for preheaders, unsubscribe markers, placeholder copy, tracking, insecure links and alt text, then renders an HTML review without sending. Trial an anonymized ESP export and compare every finding with the existing preflight.

**Evidence:** Actual MIT licence inspected; both tests passed and the synthetic CLI run produced a launch-blocked report.  
**Boundary:** Python 3.10+ with no runtime dependencies, but no ESP adapters, inbox rendering, deliverability or legal review. Reports contain campaign content, so keep them private.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/dgerridge/email-qa-framework)
