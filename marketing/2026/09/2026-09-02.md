## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Search Console · AI visibility reports and inclusion controls are global · Act**
As of 31 August, Google has rolled dedicated generative-AI visibility reports and an inclusion control out to all sites worldwide; the reports cover impressions, pages, countries, devices and dates, while the control governs links and grounding in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover. SEO and publisher teams should record each property’s inherited setting and baseline before anyone changes it, because exclusion removes AI-feature traffic and impressions.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google; the reports measure visibility, not incremental clicks or conversions.  
[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-controls-website-owners/)

**Google Ads · AI Max migration can change keyword priority and erase history · Act**
Google now says broad keywords are not treated as exact-match keywords for prioritisation when the broad-match campaign setting is disabled or a campaign moves to AI Max; converting broad terms to exact also removes their historical keyword statistics. Affected search teams should export history, add exact copies in a draft where priority matters and regression-test before migration instead of converting live terms.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google Ads Help; no performance effect has been independently measured.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15910187?hl=en)

**IAB Australia · shared campaign data becomes the measurement priority · Act**
A new review drawing on interviews across 34 companies plus buy-side surveys finds confidence drops when marketers move from single-channel measurement to understanding how channels work together; respondents want standardised campaign data that can support modelling, attribution and experiments. Agencies should align taxonomy, windows and outcome definitions across platforms before buying another measurement layer.

**Evidence:** Confirmed trade-body research; the evidence is Australian and partly qualitative, so it is a direction for operating design rather than universal performance proof.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.iabaustralia.com.au/resource/future-of-measurement-report-2026/)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**Elysium-Labs-EU/theia · Watch · Apache-2.0 · Early v0.0.13-rc.2**
This Nginx-log parser produces local SQLite pageview, referrer, status and basic visitor reports without client-side JavaScript. The sensible first trial is a synthetic log and temporary database; compare counts and restart behaviour before granting access to production logs.

**Evidence:** Tagged prerelease, source and actual Apache-2.0 licence inspected; the binary was not independently exercised.  
**Boundary:** Linux/systemd and raw Nginx-log read access are required. It stores paths, referrers and user agents plus an unsalted daily hash of IP and user agent, so treat it as pseudonymous rather than anonymous; it has no event, conversion or dashboard layer.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/Elysium-Labs-EU/theia)
