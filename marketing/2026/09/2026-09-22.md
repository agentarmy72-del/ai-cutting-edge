## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google location data · DPC orders compliance after €403m fine · Act**
Ireland’s DPC found Google breached GDPR duties across three location features and ordered compliance within six months. Audit location-based audience flows, disclosures, retention and controller roles; platform consent does not settle separate advertiser processing.

**Evidence:** Confirmed DPC decision, 21 September; processing examined was May 2018–February 2020 and the full ruling is pending.

**Google Analytics · hostname allowlists can block unapproved web sources · Test**
GA4 now lets properties allowlist approved hostnames. Inventory legitimate domains and stage the filter: Measurement Protocol bypasses it; empty hostnames are blocked.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google, 21 September; it improves property integrity but does not cover Measurement Protocol abuse.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en#09212026)

**EDPB · DSA–GDPR guidance finalised and common fining method advances · Monitor**
The EDPB finalised DSA–GDPR interplay guidance and proposed a five-step test for when fines accompany other measures. Map targeting, recommender, moderation and profiling data to both regimes, then review the published detail.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by the EDPB on 21 September; the DSA–GDPR text awaits linguistic checks and publication, while the fining-method consultation closes 13 November.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-harmonises-fining-methodology-and-adopts-final-dsa-gdpr-guidelines_en)
