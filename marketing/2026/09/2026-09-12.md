## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Ads API · access moves from developer tokens to Cloud projects · Act**

Google is shifting API sign-up and access management into Google Cloud Console, tying access levels to OAuth projects and closing pending Basic applications. Integration owners should match each project to its former token level, refresh IAM contacts and reapply where required.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google on 10 September; existing calls still work and token rejection is reserved for a future major API version.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2026/09/new-onboarding-experience-for-google-ads-api.html)

**Google Analytics · drag-and-drop Dashboards enter standard reporting · Test**

GA now lets teams assemble KPIs and new visualisations in one flexible report. Build one duplicate weekly view, reconcile every card with its source report and settle ownership before replacing an established reporting surface.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google’s 9 September release notes; no independent evidence yet that the new surface saves time or reduces reporting errors.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9164320?hl=en#09092026)

**US ad market · IAB lifts its 2026 growth forecast to 12.3% · Test**

IAB raised its projection from 9.5%; social, CTV and commerce media lead its channel outlook. Rebase US pitch and capacity scenarios, but keep a downside case rather than turning category growth into a client budget instruction.

**Evidence:** Observed in an IAB survey of 200+ brand and agency decision-makers published 10 September; it is forward-looking and partly attributes the stronger first half to the Olympics and World Cup.  
[Direct source ↗](https://www.iab.com/news/iab-raises-2026-u-s-ad-spend-forecast/)
