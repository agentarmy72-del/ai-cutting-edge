## 📡 Marketing Signals

**YouTube Ads · personalised alcohol targeting opens 30 October · Act**
Google will allow personalised alcohol ads on YouTube where local law permits; Egypt, India, Indonesia and Poland remain excluded, while age, minors and alcohol-health targeting restrictions remain. Map eligible markets and update targeting and approval checks before testing.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google; the change is not live until 30 October and no performance evidence is supplied.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17598957?hl=en)

**Google Ads · exact and phrase keywords enter a small AI Mode test · Monitor**
Google confirmed a small experiment: regular Search campaigns using exact or phrase match may serve text ads in AI Mode for explicit, direct intent. Tag the test window and watch query and conversion quality; do not move to AI Max or Performance Max merely to chase AI Mode reach.

**Evidence:** Observed across multiple campaigns and confirmed by Google’s Ads Liaison as a small experiment; scope and reporting remain unpublished.  
[Accessible report ↗](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-serving-restrictive-match-types-ai-mode-42024.html)

**Google Waze Ads · store-goal inventory expands to 42 markets · Test**
Google now lists 42 markets, including the UK, for Promoted Places in Waze through Performance Max store-goal campaigns bidding to directions, visits or sales. Test one store cohort against verified offline outcomes; the documented route is bundled into Performance Max.

**Evidence:** Confirmed in Google Ads Help; Google provides no independent evidence of incremental store lift.  
[Direct source ↗](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12971048?hl=en#Waze)

## 🛠 Marketing Tool Radar

**trypostit/trypost · Test carefully · AGPL-3.0 · Active, high-churn 1.0.9**
v1.0.9 adds HMAC-signed post-lifecycle webhooks with retry, replay and automatic pausing after five failures, then exposes their administration through REST and MCP. Send one synthetic post from an isolated workspace to a disposable receiver and force a failure before connecting approvals or reporting.

**Evidence:** Tagged release, actual AGPL licence, implementation and test files inspected; runtime behaviour was not independently exercised.  
**Boundary:** Owner/admin API or MCP credentials can manage endpoints, replay events and reveal or rotate signing secrets. Events carry post data; self-hosting concentrates social tokens and outbound access.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost/releases/tag/v1.0.9)

**formbricks/formbricks · Test carefully · AGPL-3.0 core; SDKs MIT; EE separate · Mature 5.4.2**
v5.4.2 keeps Redis connections alive, falls back to the database on Redis session errors, supports verification-token consumption on Redis 6 and translates CSAT/CES scale labels. Run a synthetic survey and verification flow through a staged Redis interruption before upgrading.

**Evidence:** Tagged stable release, source and actual split licence inspected; behaviour was not independently exercised.  
**Boundary:** Survey and contact data sit in the selected cloud or self-hosted stack; self-hosting requires PostgreSQL, Redis, authentication secrets, TLS and backups, while enterprise code has separate terms.  
[Open repository ↗](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks/releases/tag/5.4.2)
