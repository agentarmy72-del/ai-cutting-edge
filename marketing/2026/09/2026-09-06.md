## 📡 Marketing Signals

**Google Ads API · shared proxy and MCP integrations face a direct-project rule · Act**
Google now requires integrations to use their own dedicated Cloud project; shared hosted wrappers that re-expose Ads capabilities, including MCP servers, are the target. Audit reporting, bidding and AI integrations, verify direct authentication and project ownership, and seek review where a secondary interface is essential.

**Evidence:** Confirmed by Google; seven-day rollout exception because its compliance team says existing integrations are already under review.  
[Direct source ↗](https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2026/08/making-google-ads-more-secure-with.html)

**EU AI Act · Dutch agency guide turns disclosure duties into four checks · Act**
VIA Nederland maps Article 50 across consumer chatbots, realistic synthetic media, public-interest text and agency-built AI apps. Add role, use-case, visible-disclosure and substantive human-review checks to creative QA.

**Evidence:** Confirmed trade-body guidance, not a regulator ruling or legal advice; seven-day regulatory exception because Article 50 duties are live and the new workflow is actionable now.  
[Direct source ↗](https://vianederland.nl/kennisbank/ai-transparantie-voor-creatieve-bureaus-wanneer-moet-je-ai-gebruik-vermelden)

**CTV creative · buyer fear of AI ads exceeds surveyed viewer dislike · Test**
FreeWheel’s US surveys found 48% of 226 buyers expected viewers to find AI creative off-putting, while 10% of 2,496 viewers said they disliked AI ads they had seen; 50 sellers were also surveyed. Run blind brand and comprehension tests before rejecting AI creative, then judge live lift independently.

**Evidence:** Observed in a vendor-commissioned survey; self-reported, US-only and not campaign-performance proof.  
[Accessible report ↗](https://ppc.land/freewheel-finds-48-of-buyers-overrate-viewer-dislike-of-ai-ads/)
