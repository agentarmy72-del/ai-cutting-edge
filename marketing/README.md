# Marketing Edge Brief

**Question:** What changed, what reusable method emerged, and what credible software can affect an agency decision?

Marketing Edge is delivered privately but written as broad, public-safe agency intelligence. Exact delivered editions live under `YYYY/MM/`. Client names, account data, personal data/PII, confidential personal information and personalised follow-up never enter this archive.

## Three lanes

- **Marketing Signals** — material platform, algorithm, paid-media, search/discovery, measurement, privacy, commerce and strategy changes.
- **Creator Playbooks** — reusable methods from completed, completion-evidenced creator outputs only.
- **Marketing Tool Radar** — practical open-source software checked for licence, openness, setup, maintenance, data handling and material risk.

## Editorial contract

- Scan all three lanes independently; a failed lane never changes another lane's state.
- Combine five to seven qualifying cards into one private edition.
- Target two to three Signals, one to two Creator Playbooks and one to two Tool cards, but never manufacture a lane item; no normal edition may exceed three cards from one lane.
- If three or four total cards qualify, publish a daily short edition with exactly `**Short edition — <reason>**` below its date; with zero to two, roll them forward and stay silent.
- Requalify held cards before selection; expired Signals and any Creator/Tool candidate that no longer passes its evidence checks are removed rather than used as filler.
- Prefer canonical platform/regulator sources; label platform claims and independent observations accurately.
- Raw or pending creator material never qualifies.
- Stars and Trending are discovery context, not a Tool verdict; every rendered Tool card states its evidence status and setup/data/security boundary.
- Apply the repository-wide [exact briefing-footer contract](../PUBLISHING.md#briefing-footer); the footer is the final line.

## Durable material

Reusable public-safe material may receive one canonical reviewed guide under [`guides/`](guides/), indexed from the [Practical Library](../library/). Only executable, reusable and genuinely tested agent procedures route to [`library/skills/`](../library/skills/).
