## 🧩 Reusable Method
**Jake Van Clief · Verify live metadata before quoting it**
Treat a title seen inside a screenshot or older video as a dated snapshot. Reopen the canonical content ID before quoting it; in this processed reel, the live title had changed while the underlying video remained the same.

**Best for:** researchers, curators and anyone maintaining cited AI notes.  
**Evidence:** Tested by direct public read-back; this confirms metadata drift, not the creator’s wider claim about the tool.

## 🧩 Reusable Method
**Jake Van Clief · Resume interrupted projects with a four-field stage contract**
Keep one workspace per project and record: where you are, what is done, what comes next, and which decisions were already made. The last field preserves the “why” and reduces restart-by-reconstruction after an interruption.

**Best for:** agent builders and teams juggling several long-running automations.  
**Evidence:** Claimed; the wording was consistent across five recorded frames, but no working contract or before-and-after restart test was shown.  
**Verdict:** **Test**

## 🧩 Reusable Method
**Jake Van Clief · Use self-supplied visuals as a control on narration**
Before recording a capability, price or results claim, compare the voiceover with the screenshots used as proof. Here the narration framed a paid offer as a flat refusal; the displayed message showed an open negotiation.

**Best for:** anyone evaluating demos, case studies or creator-led product claims.  
**Evidence:** Demonstrated within the completed output; the screenshot was creator-supplied and its authenticity was not independently verified.
