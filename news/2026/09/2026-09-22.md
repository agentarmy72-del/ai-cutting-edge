## AI News Brief
**Tuesday, 22 September** · New frontier models land alongside a practical agent harness and consequential safety moves.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**xAI releases Grok 4.7 at Grok 4.6 pricing**
Grok 4.7 is available through the API, Cursor and Grok Build from $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. xAI **claims** stronger long-horizon coding, verification and safeguards; benchmark results are vendor-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7)

**Xiaomi releases MIT-licensed MiMo-V2.6 model weights**
Pro and Flash are native text, image, video and audio MoE models with 1M-token contexts; the smaller Flash activates 15B of 309B parameters. The weights are public and ungated, but performance comparisons are vendor-reported and deployment still needs substantial GPU capacity.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Pro-RL)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**Strands ships a batteries-included open-source agent harness**
New Apache-2.0 v0.1 packages for Python and TypeScript, plus a CLI, bundle tools, memory, sessions, limits, guardrails and tracing behind one starter interface. Developers can swap models and clouds without rebuilding the agent loop.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.com/strands-agents/harness-sdk)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**Researcher finds a Muse zero-day that can hand local processes control**
Security researcher Patrick Wardle found that an unprivileged app or terminal command can redirect transcription, capture the account token and use Muse’s broad permissions; Meta had not answered Ars when published. Mac users should treat connected accounts and device access as exposed until a fix is confirmed.

[Direct source ↗](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/muse-metas-extraordinarily-privileged-ai-assistant-has-a-serious-0-day/)

**Anthropic publishes internal measures of AI-led AI development**
Anthropic says Claude now “leads” 26% of its measured R&D work and about 30,000 internal agents operate concurrently; it plans independent third-party evaluators. The figures are self-reported and model-assisted, but offer concrete oversight metrics other labs and regulators can demand.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/measuring-pace-of-ai-development)

**New York sets dates for mandatory frontier-AI incident reporting**
Large developers must register from November, then publish safety frameworks, file quarterly catastrophic-risk assessments and report critical incidents within 72 hours from January 2027. The rollout turns the RAISE Act into an operational compliance regime.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/ai-safety-governor-hochul-announces-next-steps-regulate-major-ai-developers-and-protect-new)

**British Columbia sues OpenAI over unreported threat signals**
The province and a school district filed in California, alleging OpenAI failed to notify police of threats detected before the Tumbler Ridge shooting and designed an unsafe product. The case could test what duties AI services owe when their systems surface credible threats.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/bc-government-announce-update-openai-legal-action-9.7352395)
