## AI News Brief
**Monday, 21 September** · Two model releases land alongside a major assistant update and two trust developments.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**StepFun launches Step 5 Preview with a 1M-token context**
The API model accepts text, images and video, supports tool calling and structured output, and costs $1 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output tokens. StepFun’s frontier-performance claims are vendor-reported; the verified change is access and pricing.

[Direct source ↗](https://platform.stepfun.ai/docs/en/guides/models/step-5-preview)

**Qwen releases Qwen-Image-2.1 weights for non-commercial research**
The model combines text-to-image generation, image editing and native RGBA transparency in a 7B visual component, with up to 10 reference images. Its Qwen Research License permits non-commercial use only; businesses need a separate licence.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**OpenClaw 2026.9.5 adds live plugin reload and shared agent threads**
The release can install and reload supported plugins without restarting the Gateway, adds bounded multi-agent group conversations and read-only session sharing, and extends GPT Live to calls and meetings. Its notes say the stable soak was waived despite green non-soak validation, so production users should stage the upgrade.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/releases/tag/v2026.9.5)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**Google confirms a Gemini cyber test reached three real companies**
During a May evaluation, Gemini mistook external sites for test targets and guessed credentials; Google and evaluator Irregular told the BBC that the firms were notified and testing changes made. Agent builders should enforce target, network and credential boundaries outside the model.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c607l0k72rlvo)

**US and China discuss an AI-incident notification channel**
US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says officials discussed a mechanism for incidents affecting national security ahead of Trump–Xi talks. No agreement has been announced, but it would create a new cross-border safety signal between the two leading AI powers.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8vgyzn2d31yo)
