## AI News Brief
**Saturday, 19 September** · New multimodal and local models arrive alongside two practical agent-security actions.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**Qwen releases Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash with a 1M-token context**
The API model accepts text, images, audio and video, supports tool use, and has a Realtime variant. Qwen also open-sourced its Qwen-Live Harness and expanded its media plugins; benchmark and cost comparisons are vendor-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.8-omni-flash)

**Kimi K3 is now available on Amazon Bedrock**
AWS added Moonshot AI’s open-weight model through US and global inference profiles, with native vision, a 1M-token context window, explicit prompt caching and OpenAI-compatible APIs. This gives Bedrock users another long-context option for coding and knowledge work.

[Direct source ↗](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-kimi-k3-on-amazon-bedrock/)

**PrismML releases a 5.9GB Apache-2.0 27B model**
Bonsai 2 27B supports text and images, a 262K-token context, CUDA and Apple MLX, making a 27B-class model practical on more local hardware. PrismML **claims** it retains 98.2% of the full-precision Qwen3.8 27B model’s aggregate benchmark score.

[Direct source ↗](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-27b)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**Google opens its CC household agent to US adults**
The early experiment gives an agent its own Google account and lets up to six members selectively share email, files, calendars and tasks; with permission, it can update plans and complete forms. Existing users are being upgraded and others can join the US waitlist.

[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/cc-expanding-to-groups/)

**GitHub will remove six Copilot models on 19 October**
Gemini 3.7 Flash, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.4, GPT-5.4 mini, GPT-5 mini and Grok 4.5 will leave every Copilot experience. Users and administrators should move workflows and model policies to GitHub’s named replacements before the deadline.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models-in-mid-october/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**Plugin4Shell bypasses plugin version pins in four coding agents**
AIR disclosed a repository trick that can swap reviewed plugin code in Claude Code, Codex, Copilot and Gemini CLI; the SHA-shaped branch route affects hosts such as Bitbucket and self-hosted Git, not GitHub, while Gemini CLI has a separate variant. Update Claude Code to 2.1.179+ and Codex to 0.146.0+; AIR reports no Copilot fix and advises Gemini CLI users to migrate.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.air.security/blog-posts/plugin4shell)

**California accelerates independent AI oversight**
A new executive order speeds implementation of the state’s auditor and verification laws and asks experts to propose onsite evaluators, verified safety filings and tested shutdown controls within two months. The “kill switch” is under consideration, not yet a legal requirement.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/18/governor-newsom-issues-executive-order-to-accelerate-independent-oversight-and-advance-the-creation-of-an-ai-kill-switch/)
