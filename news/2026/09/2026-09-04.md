## AI News Brief
**Friday, 4 September** · New model access arrived alongside local-AI tooling and a major platform acquisition.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**OpenAI begins the GPT-6 Astra rollout**

Astra is rolling out over the coming days to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise, and through the OpenAI API, Azure and Bedrock; standard API pricing is $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. OpenAI says it is its first broadly deployed model at “Critical” cyber capability and reports lower monitorability under adversarial tests, so tool access and human review matter more, not less.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/)

**Google makes WeatherNext 3 available across products and Cloud**

Google’s hourly model ingests live satellite data and forecasts at up to 5km resolution, now feeding Search, Gemini, Maps, Maps Platform and Earth Engine; its data is also queryable in BigQuery or downloadable from Cloud Storage. Google reports materially better precipitation forecasts and cites independent Brightband live evaluations; users should still follow national services for official warnings.

[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-weathernext-3/)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**NVIDIA releases open-source PAIR for pooled local inference**

The free beta routes independent Ollama and LM Studio requests across compatible devices on a local network, with Windows, macOS and Linux support spanning recent NVIDIA GPUs and Apple M4-or-newer systems. It can parallelise local-agent workloads without sending those inference jobs to a cloud; NVIDIA’s accompanying performance claims are vendor-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-ifa-next-gen-agents-nv-pair-rtx-spark/)

**GitHub sets 2 October retirement for four Copilot models**

GitHub will remove Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.6 Flash, Kimi K2.7 Code and Claude Opus 4.7 from Copilot Chat, edits, agent mode and completions. Users should migrate now; Business and Enterprise admins may need to enable the suggested replacements in model policies.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-03-upcoming-deprecation-of-selected-github-copilot-models/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**NVIDIA agrees $12.93bn deal to acquire Hugging Face**

NVIDIA says Hugging Face will remain open, multi-cloud and multi-accelerator, with no NVIDIA-compute requirement. The deal shifts ownership of a central model, dataset and app hub to the leading AI-chip supplier, making those neutrality commitments worth monitoring.

[Direct source ↗](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/)

**OpenAI commits $1bn to subsidised cyber access**

OpenAI says its Daybreak commitment is intended to be consumed over six months by resource-constrained critical-infrastructure operators, local governments, banks, nonprofits and open-source maintainers. The programme offers model access, training and technical support, while more than 35 partner products and services bring Daybreak models into existing security workflows.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-for-frontline-defenders/)
