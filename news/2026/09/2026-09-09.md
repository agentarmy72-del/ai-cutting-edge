## AI News Brief
**Wednesday, 9 September** · New creative and agent access arrived alongside a major research claim and sharper model-security warnings.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**OpenAI releases ChatGPT Images 2.5 across every tier**

OpenAI made Images 2.5 available in ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex, with Flare and higher-precision Sunburst models in the API; it also added Sketch, templates and image comments. OpenAI claims up to 50% lower latency and better edit consistency—useful for creative workflows, but still vendor-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/)

**OpenAI publishes a claimed Navier–Stokes solution**

OpenAI published what it says is a solution to the 90-year-old Navier–Stokes existence-and-smoothness problem, plus a paper and Lean formalisation, after an internal system used roughly 10,000 agents. The claim and a related research-provenance dispute are not independently settled; treat this as a checkable research release, not a closed result.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/)

**Google opens AlphaGenome Atlas to researchers**

Google released a free non-commercial research portal with predictions for all nine billion possible single-letter human DNA variants and a combined impact score. It can speed variant prioritisation, but Google says it is neither clinically validated nor approved for diagnosis.

[Direct source ↗](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-atlas-a-predictive-map-of-every-possible-dna-letter-change-in-the-human-genome/)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**Meta launches Muse for cross-app personal tasks**

Meta began a US rollout of Muse on web, iOS, Android and WhatsApp: an agent that can keep working in the background, send email, book travel and purchase through Stripe Link after approval. Meta says it isolates each agent in a dedicated VM, but users should still review app scopes and the model-training opt-out before connecting sensitive services.

[Direct source ↗](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)

**Critical DeepSeek Harness flaw lets agents escape their sandbox**

OX Security disclosed CVE-2026-82533, rated 9.4: a sandboxed DeepSeek Harness agent could call an unauthenticated local control API and disable its own confinement; exposed ports also risk remote takeover and conversation export. Versions 0.1.1-rc.2 and earlier are affected; upgrade to 0.1.2-alpha.1 or later.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.ox.security/blog/cve-2026-82533-deepseek-harness-ai-agent-sandbox-escape/)

**AWS gives students at 132 universities a free year of Kiro**

AWS expanded its programme from 11 to 132 universities across 18 countries, giving eligible students a free year of Kiro Web with 1,000 monthly credits and no card. This is a concrete access change for student developers, but eligibility remains institution-limited.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/amazon-expands-free-kiro-access-students-universities)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**US agencies allege industrial-scale extraction from frontier models**

The NSA, CISA and FBI say six China-based AI companies extracted billions of tokens from Claude, GPT, Gemini and Grok through APIs, cloud services, aggregators and proxy “transfer stations.” This is a US-government attribution, not an independently adjudicated finding; the advisory recommends tighter identity checks, rate limits, telemetry and cross-company threat sharing.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a)
