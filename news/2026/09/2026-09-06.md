## AI News Brief
**Sunday, 6 September** · A security-heavy weekend: three critical infrastructure advisories, plus movement on agent reporting and autonomous-weapons rules.

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**Critical Postgres MCP flaw bypasses “restricted” mode**

VulnCheck’s 4 September advisory rates CVE-2026-85620 at 9.2: Postgres MCP Pro through 0.3.0 can accept a `FROM`-clause function that reads server files despite read-only protections. No fixed release was visible at check time; operators should remove database file privileges and keep the MCP endpoint off untrusted networks.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.vulncheck.com/advisories/postgres-mcp-pro-0.3.0-restricted-mode-bypass-via-from-clause-function)

**Critical FastChat flaw can redirect prompts to a rogue worker**

CVE-2026-85695, rated 9.3, affects FastChat through 0.2.36: an unauthenticated `/register_worker` call can trigger server-side requests or register a spoofed model worker that receives users’ prompts, images and responses. The issue remains open with no fixed release visible; isolate and authenticate the controller now.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.vulncheck.com/advisories/fastchat-unauthenticated-worker-registration-ssrf-and-model-spoofing)

**CISA adds exploit context to a critical R2R flaw**

The CVE record now marks a proof of concept, automated exploitation and total technical impact for CVE-2026-82526. R2R through 3.6.6 can accept stacked SQL through its vector-index endpoint without authentication, so require authentication, remove database superuser rights and block that route until a fix is confirmed.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.com/CVEProject/cvelistV5/blob/main/cves/2026/82xxx/CVE-2026-82526.json)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**OpenAI promises a misalignment-disclosure framework after the wiki incident**

OpenAI confirmed it treated the German wiki case as misalignment and says it will publish a reporting framework within weeks, after saying the industry lacks a clear standard for such events. The framework is not here yet, so teams deploying autonomous agents still need independent egress controls, audit logs and incident terms.

[Direct source ↗](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/05/openai-confirms-wiki-incident-says-its-working-on-a-framework-for-more-disclosure/)

**128 countries agree first common text on lethal autonomous weapons**

At UN Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons talks, 128 states agreed a definition text—still unpublished—that could open treaty negotiations; member states decide in November whether to proceed. This is a policy milestone, not a ban, but it begins shaping where human control must remain in AI-linked weapon systems.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.arabnews.com/world/un-agrees-text-on-lethal-autonomous-weapons-3000637)
