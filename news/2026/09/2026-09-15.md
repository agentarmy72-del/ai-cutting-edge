## AI News Brief
**Tuesday, 15 September** · Local agents gain wider access while new controls arrive for model choice, delegated access and model behaviour.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**Apple starts rolling out Siri AI**

Apple has begun an English-language beta rollout on compatible devices running its 2027 software releases, adding personal-context search across messages, email and photos, onscreen awareness, web answers and wider app actions. This is the availability change announced last week; French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish are due next month.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**Perplexity brings its local Computer agent to Windows RTX PCs**

Perplexity’s local Computer agent is now in its Windows app for GeForce RTX and RTX PRO GPUs with at least 24GB of VRAM. NVIDIA says local work keeps files on-device and avoids Computer credits, while cloud escalation asks permission; the hardware floor is the catch.

[Direct source ↗](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/local-ai-perplexity-windows-pcs/)

**GitHub adds cost-and-quality tiers to Copilot’s automatic model picker**

Copilot is rolling out efficiency, balance and intelligence tiers in VS Code, Copilot CLI and the Copilot app. The tier steers cost, latency and quality rather than pinning one model; usage is still billed according to whichever model Copilot selects.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-14-configure-cost-and-quality-in-copilot-auto-model-selection/)

**AWS removes custom OAuth plumbing from AgentCore agents**

AgentCore Identity now supplies a managed Consent portal and session-binding endpoint for AgentCore Gateway, replacing callback infrastructure teams previously had to host themselves. Users can approve providers separately, AWS stores tokens in its vault, and consent operations are visible in CloudTrail.

[Direct source ↗](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/manage-end-user-oauth-consent-for-ai-agents-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**Open Secure AI Alliance moves under Linux Foundation governance**

The NVIDIA-founded alliance has moved to a neutral home for developing open defenses and standards across models, agents, identity and infrastructure. Its SAFE incident-sharing proposal is open for comment until 21 September; this is an RFC and governance step, not a finished defense stack.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance-joins-the-linux-foundation-to-build-a-shared-open-defense-stack-for-the-ai-era)

**Microsoft opens its proposed rules for MAI models to public review**

Microsoft AI published a draft code it intends to use to train and govern MAI models from 2027, including rules against resisting interruption or shutdown, expanding their own scope, or hiding reasoning from auditors. The six-week consultation is a stated future policy—not evidence of current model behaviour.

[Direct source ↗](https://microsoft.ai/pdf/MAI_CodeOfConduct.pdf)
