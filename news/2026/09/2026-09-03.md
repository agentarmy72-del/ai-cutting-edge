## AI News Brief
**Thursday, 3 September** · New models arrived alongside tighter controls over what agents can see, do and retain.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**Google launches Gemini 3.8 Flash and a restricted cyber variant**

Gemini 3.8 Flash is available across Google’s developer, enterprise and consumer products at introductory API rates of $0.75/$3.75 per million input/output tokens; those rates double on 1 January 2027. Google claims stronger coding and agentic reasoning, while the more permissive Cyber variant is limited to vetted defenders through Fairwind.

[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/)

**Meta releases Muse Spark 1.3 for coding and agentic work**

Meta is rolling the model out in Muse Code and Meta Model API, claiming better long-task execution, multitasking and prompt-injection resistance. Max reasoning awaits further safety testing, and promised open weights are not yet available.

[Direct source ↗](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3)

**Meta launches real-time Muse Voice Transcribe**

Available through Meta Model API, Meta AI for Mac and Muse Code, the model combines streaming transcription, endpointing, code-switching and live separation of 20-plus speakers. Meta extensively validated 25 of its 70-plus training languages; benchmark leads are Meta-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-voice-transcribe)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**GitHub Copilot app and CLI now obey content exclusions**

For Copilot Business and Enterprise, administrator policies can now keep excluded files out of context in the Copilot app and CLI. Teams using agentic workflows outside the IDE should verify that sensitive-code exclusions are configured at the right enterprise, organisation or repository level.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-02-content-exclusions-generally-available-in-copilot-app-and-cli/)

**Hugging Face publishes 207 WebGPU kernels for browser AI**

The Apache-2.0 preview packages versioned GPU operations with correctness and benchmark cases, alongside a browser-based testing tool called Fleet. Web and local-AI developers can load reusable browser-native operations, although support still depends on the browser, operating system, GPU and driver.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/webgpu-kernels)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**CrowdStrike and OpenAI add runtime controls for Codex agents**

The companies announced Falcon Guardian inventory, telemetry and policy enforcement for supported Codex agents, plus planned use of GPT-5.6 Cyber first in CrowdStrike’s FAIRR service and later across Falcon. This creates a concrete monitoring route for enterprise agents, but broader GPT-5.6 Cyber timing was not specified.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-and-openai-expand-partnership-to-secure-the-agentic-era/)

**Anthropic previews customer-controlled Enterprise Frontier Safeguards**

Due to roll out in phases later this fall, EFS will keep monitoring data in customer-controlled cloud storage and use automated cross-session misuse detection without Anthropic staff reviewing flagged content. Anthropic will not charge for EFS, though cloud storage and transfer costs remain; eligible customers receive zero-data-retention access until rollout.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.anthropic.com/news/enterprise-frontier-safeguards)
