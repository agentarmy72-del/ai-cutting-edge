## AI News Brief
**Wednesday, 23 September** · Prices fall while agent builders gain stronger local execution, observability and evaluation.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms

**OpenAI launches GPT‑6 Sol and Luna with 50% lower API prices**
Sol costs $2/$10 per million input/output tokens and Luna $0.10/$0.50—half their GPT‑5.6 promotional pricing. Both are now in the API and ChatGPT Work/Codex; GPT‑6 caching adds discounts of up to 90% on cached input plus diagnostics and explicit breakpoints.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna)

**Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5 at lower cost**
Input/output pricing falls to $4/$20 per million tokens and cache reads to $0.20, respectively 20% and 60% below Opus 5. Anthropic **claims** output is over 30% faster and typical task costs are 40% lower.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source

**Hugging Face Transformers can now load llama.cpp GGUF quants directly**
Developers can pass a Hub GGUF file to `from_pretrained`, keep the standard Transformers API and expose an OpenAI-compatible local server. Accelerated quantized loading initially targets Apple Silicon; unsupported kernels fall back to dequantization and need more memory.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/blog/transformers-llama-cpp-quants)

**GitHub adds OpenTelemetry tracing for Copilot agents**
Enterprise admins can centrally export model calls, tool activity and step-by-step agent traces to compatible monitoring systems. Prompt and response content is excluded by default, giving teams useful diagnostics without collecting conversation text unless they opt in.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-22-opentelemetry-in-the-github-copilot-app)

**AWS adds tests for agent skill selection and instruction-following**
Strands Evals and Bedrock AgentCore can now score whether an agent chose the right `SKILL.md`, followed its steps and loaded a required skill. Launch support spans Strands, Claude tooling, OpenAI Agents and Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenHands and Google ADK, making these checks usable in CI.

[Direct source ↗](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/evaluate-skill-equipped-agents-with-strands-evals-and-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust

**Meta says it hotfixed the Muse account-hijack zero-day**
Ars reports that Meta released a hotfix more than 12 hours after the flaw was disclosed; no patched build number is named. The immediate risk is reduced in principle, but Muse users should verify their app is updated before relying on its connected-account access.

[Direct source ↗](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/muse-metas-extraordinarily-privileged-ai-assistant-has-a-serious-0-day/)

**OpenAI commits to deep-access independent safety assessments**
OpenAI says assessors should be able to examine training, evaluations, deployments, safeguards and critical misalignment incidents. This is a framework and commitment—not evidence of completed audits—so the next proof is named assessors, published methods and findings.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/priorities-principles-third-party-assessments)
