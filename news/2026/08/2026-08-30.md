## AI News Brief
**Sunday, 30 August** · Two major open-weight releases landed alongside practical account, usage and billing changes.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**Tencent opens Hy4 preview under Apache 2.0**

Hy4 preview provides a 770B-parameter MoE model with 49B active parameters and a 1M-token context. Tencent’s performance claims are vendor-reported, and the model’s size means most users will need hosted access rather than local deployment.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-preview)

**Z.ai publishes GLM-5.3 weights under a custom licence**

GLM-5.3 can now be downloaded, customized and commercially deployed. Its licence requires model-as-a-service groups earning more than $10bn over 12 months to pass a Z.ai security review; performance claims remain vendor-reported.

[Direct source ↗](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/GLM-5.3)

**ChatGPT connects multiple Google accounts**

ChatGPT can now use multiple Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts accounts in one conversation on supported Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans worldwide. Check which account and permissions are active before approving actions.

[Direct source ↗](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#connect-multiple-google-accounts-to-chatgpt)

**Gemini Notebook moves to compute-based usage limits**

From 2 September, consumer accounts will receive limits that refresh every five hours and vary with prompt complexity, chat length, source count and feature use. Heavier outputs such as Video Overviews and Slide Decks can be queued for later generation.

[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/new-flexible-usage-limits/)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**OpenAI plans to end model supply to Cursor**

OpenAI proposes maintaining Cursor’s current model access until 12 November but will not provide future models; the final termination date remains unconfirmed. Personal API keys can preserve access in local Chat and Agent features, but not Tab, Auto, cloud agents, automations, CLI, API or SDK.

[Direct source ↗](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001506-using-openai-models-in-cursor)

**Claude Code sets a 17% cut from current weekly limits**

Anthropic says standard weekly limits will rise 25% from baseline on 14 September, replacing the temporary 50% uplift. Compared with today, Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise users will receive 17% less weekly capacity.

[Direct source ↗](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2093742322525810912)

**GitHub changes Copilot business billing and access defaults**

From 1 October, eligible Business and Enterprise seats paid by card or PayPal will be charged upfront, without prorated refunds when seats are revoked. A unified Copilot experience will become the default no earlier than 28 September, so administrators should review policies and spend controls.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)
