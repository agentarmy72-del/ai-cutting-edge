## AI News Brief
**Friday, 21 August** · Messaging actions, model routing, multiplayer agents and security controls move into practical use.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**OpenAI adds Apple Messages actions to Codex and ChatGPT Work**
OpenAI’s new macOS plug-in can read, search and send iMessage, SMS and RCS from Codex or ChatGPT Work on Apple-silicon Macs; it is available on all plans but not in regular ChatGPT chats. Sends require approval by default—keep that control on wherever messages may contain untrusted instructions.
[Direct source ↗](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/plugins?surface=app)

**Ramp opens a 26-model routing API**
Ramp Router gives US developers one compatible endpoint across 26 models, with routing fees waived through 2026 while inference remains billable; Ramp’s claimed 40% average cost reduction is a vendor figure. The service stores inputs, outputs and metadata, so review its retention controls before sending sensitive data.
[Direct source ↗](https://router.com/)

**Google gives users finer control over AI-mediated discovery**
Google now lets users mark preferred publishers for Search, AI Overviews and AI Mode, while natural-language Discover tuning is rolling out and Android News users can curate audio briefings. Readers gain more source control, and publishers gain a direct route back into AI-mediated results.
[Direct source ↗](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**OpenAI documents Codex’s open harness as an embeddable platform**
OpenAI has published a platform guide covering its open-source CLI, app-server and SDK, with state, tool use, sandboxing and approval flows exposed to host applications. Developers can embed the agent loop into specialised products instead of rebuilding the runtime.
[Direct source ↗](https://developers.openai.com/blog/codex-as-a-platform)

**Slack launches multiplayer code channels for AI agents**
Slack Code gives teams dedicated channels to direct coding agents, inspect diffs and previews, and approve changes; Slack also added an Agents tab and one-click deployment from ten partner platforms. This brings shared context and admin controls into agentic coding, but production changes still need expert sign-off.
[Direct source ↗](https://www.salesforce.com/introducing-slack-code/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**UK NCSC issues interim security guidance for agentic AI**
The NCSC tells system designers to minimise unnecessary autonomy, add human oversight, sandbox agents, log and attribute their actions, and retain an emergency shutdown path. Teams deploying autonomous workflows now have a practical baseline while formal guidance is developed.
[Direct source ↗](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/blogs/managing-the-cyber-risk-of-agentic-ai)
