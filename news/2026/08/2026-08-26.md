## AI News Brief
**Wednesday, 26 August** · New inference hardware, industry agents and agent-security controls moved forward.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**OpenAI publishes first results for its Jalapeño inference chip**

OpenAI claims 1.5–1.9× more work per watt and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency than comparison systems across three open models, with deployment inside OpenAI planned by year-end. These are vendor-run benchmarks, but custom silicon could change future inference capacity and cost.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results/)

**Google previews Gemini Enterprise editions for legal and finance**

Google Cloud has introduced purpose-built agent packages for legal and financial-services workflows, with domain skills, governed connectors and both editions initially in preview. Regulated teams gain a more packaged route to agents, but should validate the vendor’s capability and control claims against their own work.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-08-25-Google-Cloud-Launches-Gemini-Enterprise-for-Financial-Services)

**NVIDIA previews Jetson Orin Nano 2 for edge AI**

NVIDIA claims twice its predecessor’s inference performance and 40% lower power at matched performance, with the module and developer kit due in the first half of 2027. Robotics and local-vision builders gain a clearer upgrade path, but this is advance availability—not hardware they can buy yet.

[Direct source ↗](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-jetson-orin-nano-2-robotics-computer-to-redefine-entry-level-edge-ai)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**GitHub Copilot’s Customize tab is generally available**

The Copilot app now gathers MCP servers, plugins, skills and canvases into one discovery surface. Teams can find integrations more easily, but should still review permissions and publisher trust before connecting tools.

[Direct source ↗](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-25-github-copilot-app-customize-tab-is-generally-available/)

**Okta makes Agent SSO generally available**

Okta has folded the open Cross App Access standard into core SSO at no added cost, registering compatible agents as managed identities with policy and audit records. Enterprise teams can replace some static-key connections with centrally governed access; only supported agents receive this path.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.okta.com/newsroom/press-releases/okta-brings-first-class-identity-to-ai-agents-with-agent-sso/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**NemoClaw’s Windows/WSL Ollama path faces a model-poisoning disclosure**

Security researchers report a DNS-rebinding path that lets a malicious webpage reach NemoClaw’s unauthenticated Windows/WSL Ollama service and plant persistent model-template instructions. The report says no fix existed for that path on 25 August, with no CVE or observed exploitation; verify platform-specific mitigation before attaching sensitive tools.

[Direct source ↗](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/a-malicious-webpage-could-poison-your.html)

**OpenAI disrupts a Russia-linked covert influence operation**

OpenAI says it banned accounts linked to a campaign that promoted a purported institute, republished copied or misattributed academic work, and seeded social posts across several platforms; it assesses the operation’s reach as limited. The case shows AI supporting a broader credibility operation, so check institutions and source authorship—not just text style.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-influence-campaign-russia/)
