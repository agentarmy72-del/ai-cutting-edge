## AI News Brief
**Tuesday, 25 August** · New model access, open agent discovery and state-level scrutiny landed together.

## 🧠 Models & Platforms
**GPT‑5.6 arrives in Kiro**

OpenAI says Sol, Terra and Luna are now available inside AWS’s Kiro coding agent. Its roughly 82% Terra cost reduction on Terminal-Bench 2.1 is a joint vendor test, so benchmark quality and spend on your own repositories.

[Direct source ↗](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6-in-kiro/)

**Thomson Reuters launches its own professional-domain model**

Thomson will first enter CoCounsel Legal’s Tabular Analysis, while a smaller open-weight version is available for academic and non-commercial use. Claims of frontier-level quality and lower costs remain vendor and early-evaluator results, so treat them as preliminary.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2026/august/thomson-reuters-leverages-its-world-class-data-assets-to-launch-its-own-frontier-model)

**NVIDIA starts full production of Groq 3 LPX**

NVIDIA says its agent-focused inference accelerator is now in full production, with Nebius first to adopt it alongside Vera Rubin. Speed and efficiency figures are vendor benchmarks; practical access will depend on cloud availability and pricing.

[Direct source ↗](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-groq-3-lpx-now-in-full-production-with-world-class-speed-for-agentic-ai)

## 🛠 Agents, Tools & Open Source
**Open ARD protocol targets cross-platform agent discovery**

ARD defines a federated way to discover agents, MCP servers, skills, APIs and workflows without replacing their execution protocols. It is an early Apache-licensed specification, so builders can test it now but should not assume ecosystem-wide support.

[Direct source ↗](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/agentic-resource-discovery-ard-an-open-specification-for-agent-discovery/)

**SageMaker HyperPod adds managed Ray workflows**

AWS has added Ray cluster creation, notebooks, dashboards, job submission and fault recovery to SageMaker HyperPod on EKS, using standard Ray APIs. Existing HyperPod users can shed Kubernetes setup, but underlying compute remains billable.

[Direct source ↗](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-new-ray-capabilities-on-sagemaker-hyperpod/)

## ⚖️ Industry, Policy & Trust
**Alabama subpoenas OpenAI over the Hugging Face security incident**

The Alabama attorney general issued a subpoena and opened a consumer-protection investigation into OpenAI’s oversight of the Hugging Face incident. This is an investigation, not a finding of wrongdoing; agent operators should preserve authorization controls and audit evidence.

[Direct source ↗](https://www.alabamaag.gov/attorney-general-marshall-launches-investigation-into-openai-and-sam-altman-for-massive-artificial-intelligence-data-breach/)
